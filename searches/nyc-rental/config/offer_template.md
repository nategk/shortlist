# Contact Template — NYC rental, Oct 1 move-in

## Current template

Hi — I'm interested in [ADDRESS / UNIT] listed at $[PRICE]. I'm looking
for an October 1 lease start and could view as soon as [DAY/TIME].

A few quick questions:
- Is the unit still available for an October 1 (or earlier) start?
- Are the photos of this exact unit? Could you send a floor plan or video?
- What floor is it on, and is it an elevator building?
- Is there a bike room or somewhere to store a bike inside?
- Move-in costs: first month + security only, or anything else?
  (Understanding under the FARE Act that a landlord-hired broker's fee
  isn't charged to the tenant.)

I have [income/credit/guarantor summary] and can apply same-day.

Thanks,
[Name] · [phone]

**Adjust per listing:**
- Price mismatch between reposts → ask which is current.
- No street address → ask for the block and nearest cross street before
  booking a viewing.

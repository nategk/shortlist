# Target List (Template)

Copy into your project folder (e.g. `car/target_list.md`). Specific
models/units to watch for and prioritize when scanning sources — not a
strict allow-list, just where to look first. A listing outside this list can
still score well against `criteria.md` if it fits the type/fit/spec rules.
